Real Estate Lead Conversion System

Problem

Leads arrive from completely different channels — property portal emails (99acres, MagicBricks), the website, Instagram ads — with no consistent format and no way to tell a serious buyer from a casual browser. By the time an agent manually reads, scores and replies to each one, a faster competitor has already booked the viewing.

Solution

A 5-workflow n8n system: leads are captured from every channel, deduplicated and rule-scored, then handed to a qualification bot built on Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API that holds a real qualifying conversation. Qualified leads enter a nurture sequence, hot leads get a real Google Calendar site-visit booked automatically, and a reporting workflow rolls everything up for the agent daily. Every piece was tested against real Meta and Google infrastructure — real WhatsApp messages sent and answered, a real calendar event created — not mock data.

Buyer-Listing Matching for a Real Estate Broker

Problem

A prospective client — a real estate broker — didn't want another workflow diagram. She needed proof it could handle her actual mess: a photo of a paper sign-in sheet from an open house, and a daily email digest of off-market listings buried in ads and irrelevant text, both re-typed into her CRM by hand while she raced to match buyers to the right off-market property before a public listing site got there first.

Solution

A 3-workflow system built to her exact scenario: a form-uploaded photo of the sign-in sheet is read by Gemini Vision to pull buyer name and contact straight into a buyer list; a Gmail-triggered workflow watches for the off-market digest email and uses Gemini to parse out only the real listing data from the surrounding ad content; a third workflow matches buyers against private listings and fires an alert the instant there's a fit — no manual approval step, since she explicitly rejected one on the call.

Automated Cold Outreach Pipeline

Problem

Running cold outbound well means stitching together several tools — sourcing leads, enriching them with real contact emails, sending a multi-step sequence without spamming, and stopping it the instant someone replies or opts out. Normally that means paying for Clay, Instantly, or similar.

Solution

A self-built pipeline with two lead sources feeding one system: Firecrawl pulls business name and email straight out of search-result snippets (cheaper than scraping full pages), while a separate n8n workflow hits the Google Places API for local businesses by city, dedupes them, and scrapes each website for a contact email with a headless-render fallback for JS-heavy sites. A daily sender workflow then runs an actual drip cadence — Day 0, Day 3, and a Day 7 "breakup" email — computing which stage each lead is due for and sending with spacing to avoid spam flags. A second workflow watches the inbox for replies, classifies each one as engaged or stop, and updates the lead's status so the sender automatically skips anyone who responded.

AI Receptionist for a Home Services Business

Problem

A home services business (the kind that lives or dies by picked-up calls — plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, repairs) was missing bookings every time a call came in after hours, mid-job, or during a rush. A missed call almost always means a lost customer to whoever answered first.

Solution

A custom AI voice receptionist built on Vapi that picks up every call instantly, holds a natural spoken conversation, qualifies what the customer needs, and books or routes the job — live 24/7, no hold music, no voicemail.
