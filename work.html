Real Estate Lead Intake & CRM Matching Automation

Problem

Leads for the business come in through three completely different channels — WhatsApp messages, email replies, and Google Form submissions — each in its own format. Manually reading every one, checking whether it's a duplicate, and typing it into the CRM by hand is slow, inconsistent, and leads to missed or duplicated follow-ups.

Solution

An n8n pipeline that pulls in leads from WhatsApp, the team's email inbox, and Google Form submissions in real time, uses AI to read and standardize each lead's details — name, contact info, property interest, budget, source — matches and deduplicates it against existing records, and automatically creates or updates the correct contact inside Follow Up Boss (FUB). Every lead lands in the CRM correctly tagged and matched, no matter which channel it came through, ready for the sales team to follow up immediately.

AI Receptionist for a Home Services Business

Problem

A home services business (the kind that lives or dies by picked-up calls — plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, repairs) was missing bookings every time a call came in after hours, mid-job, or during a rush. A missed call almost always means a lost customer to whoever answered first.

Solution

A custom AI voice receptionist built on Vapi that picks up every call instantly, holds a natural spoken conversation, qualifies what the customer needs, and books or routes the job — live 24/7, no hold music, no voicemail.
