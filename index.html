How I Work

How I build your system.

A transparent, engineering-first process. No black boxes, no "trust me" — you see the logic before it ever touches your business.

I audit your current manual process, find where the time actually goes, and map the exact conversation flow, data logic and decision points the system needs — before writing a single workflow node.
